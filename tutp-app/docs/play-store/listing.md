# Play Store listing text

Limits (Google Play): title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Counts below were checked by script (`tests/unit/android-prep.test.js` re-checks them). **A native Telugu speaker and a native Hindi speaker should read the Telugu and Hindi text before it is pasted into Play Console** (founder list). Wording avoids claims the product cannot back today: no "#1", no "free" promise, and no "Parent Engagement Score" (the live score is only a 14-day homework-completion rate, see CLAUDE.md backlog). Plans are not described as "free" or priced here until the Play Billing decision is made.

Keywords from `TUTP-ASO-SEO-ANDROID-STRATEGY.md` that are honestly true today and used naturally: homework help, parents, Telugu, Hindi, quiz, storytelling, question paper (teachers), State Board / CBSE.

## English (default language)

**Title** (29): `Tut-P: Parent Homework Helper`

**Short description** (76): `Homework help for parents in Telugu, Hindi or English: explain, quiz, story.`

**Full description**

```
Tut-P helps parents help their children with homework, even when the subject or the language of the school is new to them.

Take a photo of a homework question or type it. Tut-P explains it step by step in Telugu, Hindi or English, so you and your child can understand it together.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Homework Help: a clear explanation and answer for a typed or photographed question. On a photo of your child's work, Tut-P can point to the mistakes.
• Storytelling: turn a lesson into a short illustrated story in the language you choose.
• Quiz and Play-Based Learning: quick quizzes the whole family can play together.
• Experiential Learning: hands-on activities to do at home, linked to the lesson.
• Family page: add the other parent and family members, and see homework and progress in one place.
• For teachers: tools to prepare a question paper and lesson material.

MADE FOR INDIAN FAMILIES
Choose your child's class and curriculum (State Board, CBSE, ICSE and others) when you register. Explanations can be in Telugu, Hindi or English.

SIMPLE AND SAFE
You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code. One account covers the whole family, and each child has their own profile. There are no ads.

PLEASE NOTE
Explanations are written by AI and can contain mistakes. Check important answers with your child's teacher.

Privacy policy: tutp.online/privacy
Questions: contact@tutp.online
```

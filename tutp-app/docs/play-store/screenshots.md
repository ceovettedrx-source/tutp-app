# Screenshot shot list (5 screens) and feature graphic spec

Strategy rule (TUTP-ASO-SEO-ANDROID-STRATEGY.md 3.1): screenshots show a **result**, not just interface. Take them from the installed Android app (or Chrome on a phone at 1080 x 1920), with a demo family made for this, never a real family. Do not show real children's faces or names.

**Format**: PNG or JPEG, 9:16 portrait, 1080 x 1920 px (Play accepts 320-3840 px per side, longest side at most 2x the shortest). Minimum 2, maximum 8 per language; we make 5. No alpha. Add a short caption bar at the top if you want captions (text below), in the language of the listing. Keep adult-facing (see `target-audience.md`).

## Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 px

- PNG or JPEG, no alpha, under 15 MB.
- Background: Tut-P blue `#005bbf`, plain or a very soft gradient. Left third: the app icon (`public/images/icons/icon-512.png`) at about 220 px. Right two thirds: one line, 56-64 px, white, bold: **"Homework help for parents"** (te: "తల్లిదండ్రుల కోసం హోంవర్క్ సహాయం", hi: "माता-पिता के लिए होमवर्क मदद"), and underneath in 32 px: "Telugu · Hindi · English".
- Safe area: keep all text and the icon inside the centre 924 x 400 px; Play crops edges on some surfaces and shows a play-button overlay when a video is attached (no video planned).
- Not allowed by Play: price or "free" claims, "best / #1", "download now", store badges, rating stars, and anything that looks like Play UI. Do not use photos of children.
- Make one graphic per language only if the text differs; Play shows the default-language graphic to other languages otherwise.

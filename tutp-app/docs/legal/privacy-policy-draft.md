Tut-P (tutp.online) is operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, [REGISTERED ADDRESS], India ("Tut-P", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written for parents and guardians, because Tut-P accounts are created and managed by adults on behalf of their children.

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Parents and guardians** who register a family account.
- **Family members** (for example grandparents) whom a parent invites.
- **Children** whose learning is supported through the account. Children do not create their own accounts. A parent registers each child and gives consent on the child's behalf.
- **Tutors** who list themselves in the Tut-P tutor directory.

## 2. What we collect

**From parents and family members**
- Name, phone number, email address, and relationship to the child.
- Login information (one-time passwords are used to sign in; we do not store passwords).
- Answers you choose to give in surveys, such as how often your family does homework or eats together.

**About each child (entered by the parent)**
- First name, class/grade, school board, and medium of instruction.
- Homework you share with Tut-P: typed questions, photos, or PDFs of homework pages.
- Learning activity: which features were used, quiz results, completed homework, and feedback you give on answers.

**Payments**
- Plan purchased, amount, and payment status. Card, UPI, and bank details are handled by our payment processor, Razorpay. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

**Technical information**
- Basic device and browser information and error logs, used to keep the service working and secure.

We do **not** collect a child's photo, voice, precise location, or contact details, and we ask you not to include them in homework uploads.

## 3. Why we use it

- To provide homework explanations, quizzes, stories, learning activities, and reports.
- To show your family's progress and the Bonding Report.
- To process payments, manage your plan, and handle refunds.
- To connect you with a tutor when you request and pay for a contact.
- To keep Tut-P safe, prevent misuse, and fix problems.
- To improve the accuracy of our answers. Answers to common homework questions may be reused for other families, but **only the question and our explanation are reused, never your child's name or any personal details.**

We do **not** sell personal data. We do **not** show advertising to children, and we do not use children's data for advertising or profiling.

## 4. AI processing

Tut-P uses artificial intelligence services to read homework and generate explanations. When you submit homework, the question content (text, photo, or PDF) is sent to our AI provider for processing. **We do not send your child's name to the AI provider.** Our AI provider processes this content to return an answer to us and is not permitted to use it for any other purpose under our agreement with them. ## 6. Parental consent and children's data

A parent or legal guardian must be at least 18 years old to create an account and must give consent before any child's information is processed. By registering a child, you confirm that you are the child's parent or legal guardian and consent to the processing described here.

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (section 9). We will then stop processing that child's data and delete it, as described in section 7.

## 7. How long we keep data

- Ask us to delete data and close your account.
- Withdraw consent.
- Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf.
- Raise a grievance with us, and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

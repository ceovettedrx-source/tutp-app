Who we are

Tut-P (tutp.online and the Tut-P Android app) is a learning-support service for parents and children, run from Hyderabad, India. Legal entity and registered address: [TO BE FILLED BY THE FOUNDER]. Contact for anything in this policy: contact@tutp.online.

Accounts are created by adults (parents or guardians). A child does not sign up alone: children use Tut-P through the signed-in account of a parent or family member.

What we collect

AboutWhat
Parents and family membersName, date of birth, education, phone number, email address, relationship to the child, an optional photo. Family members added by a parent: name, relationship, phone number (and a password, stored only as a one-way hash, if one is set).
Each childName, date of birth, class, curriculum (board), school name, mandal and village of the school, optional full address, optional photo, favourite subject, favourite game and hobbies, and the plan chosen. Learning scores per subject that the app calculates from use.
The familyCountry, state, district, mandal and area.
Homework and learning contentThe text, photos or PDF pages you type or attach for Homework Help, Storytelling, Notes, Quiz, question papers and similar features. These are sent for processing (see "Who receives data"). We did not find code that saves the question or photo of a Homework Help request in our database; files you attach through the upload button on the website or registration are stored (see below).
Uploaded filesPhotos and documents sent through the upload feature (for example a child photo at registration, or a homework attachment on the website) are stored in our file storage and are opened by a web link. The link is long and random, but anyone who has it can open the file.
How the app is usedWhich feature was used and when, the language, how long a session took, answer feedback (including any free text you type in the feedback box), quiz results, homework completion, and reports of a wrong picture. Search-bar choices are logged with the class band, board, language and a scrambled (hashed) family code; a typed phrase kept for these is cleaned of names and deleted after 30 days.
PaymentsPlan, amount, status and Razorpay order and payment numbers. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's own checkout; we never see or store them.
Teachers and tutorsName, phone number, subjects, school, class sections, and for teachers an ID-card photo used for verification. Tutors listed in the directory: name, photo, subjects, area, bio and phone number (the number is not shown to parents).
Marketing sourceA referral code and the campaign the visitor came from (utm source, medium, campaign), saved with the registration.
Technical dataIP address and request details in our hosting logs; browser storage described below.

We do not collect a child's voice, precise location (GPS) or contacts, and the app does not ask for those permissions.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To give explanations, quizzes, stories and notes, and to show a family's progress and reports.
  • To take payments, give plans and handle refunds.
  • To send homework alerts and support emails.
  • To keep the service working, safe and fair (limits per plan, abuse checks) and to improve answers.

We do not sell personal data. The app shows no advertising and has no advertising SDK.

AI-written content

Explanations, quizzes, stories and notes are written by AI and can be wrong. Please check important answers with your child's teacher.

Cookies and similar storage

  • tutp_session: keeps you signed in (up to 30 days of inactivity). Needed for the service.
  • tutp_logged_out: remembers when you last logged out, so an old session cannot come back.
  • tutp_demo: counts how many free tries a browser has used on the home page (2 days).
  • Browser storage (sessionStorage and localStorage): the selected child, language, last screen, referral and campaign values. Stays on your device.
  • Google Analytics cookies on the home page only.

Cookies are not used for advertising.

Your choices: access, correction, deletion

Write to contact@tutp.online from the email address or phone number you registered with. To delete your account and your children's data, follow the steps on the Delete your account page. A parent can also remove a family member from the Family page.

Children

Tut-P is meant to be used by a child together with a parent or guardian. Only an adult creates the account, enters the child's details and, at registration, ticks the parent or guardian confirmation box.

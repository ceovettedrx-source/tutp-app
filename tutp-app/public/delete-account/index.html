How to ask

  1. Send an email to contact@tutp.online with the subject Delete my Tut-P account.
  2. Write from the email address you registered with, or include the phone number you sign in with. We will check that you own the account before deleting anything [HOW WE VERIFY: TO BE DECIDED].
  3. Say whether you want the whole family account deleted (parents, children, family members) or only one child's data.

Today deletion is done by our team by hand: there is no delete button in the app yet. We will reply to confirm, and aim to finish within [30] days.

What is deleted

  • The family account: parents' and family members' details, each child's profile, learning scores and progress, usage records linked to the family, and the uploaded photos and files we can link to your account.
  • Your sign-in: after deletion you can no longer sign in with that phone number.

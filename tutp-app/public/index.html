Why parents fall out of the loop

It's rarely that parents don't want to help.

It's that classwork arrives in a language, a subject and a pace that wasn't built for the parent sitting across the table. Tut-P closes three specific gaps every week.

Gap 01 — Language

The classroom speaks one language, home speaks another

Tut-P re-explains the lesson in the language you're most comfortable in, so you can guide your child without a translation app in between.

Gap 02 — Subject knowledge

It's been a while since you did long division

You don't need to relearn the syllabus. Tut-P breaks the concept down to exactly what's needed to help with tonight's homework — nothing more.

Gap 03 — Time

Ten minutes is realistic. An hour rarely is.

Every session is designed to fit into a real evening — one explanation, one short quiz, one small win — not another long homework battle.

The part every other app skips

Most apps track how your child is doing. Tut-P tracks how present you are.

Every homework app on the market produces a report card for the kid. Tut-P flips that: your Bonding Dashboard is a report card for the parent — because a child's outcomes are shaped less by one more worksheet, and more by a parent who reliably shows up.

  • A weekly bar chart of subjects you guided together, not just what your child got right.
  • A visible streak that rewards showing up consistently, even on short, imperfect days.
  • A plain-language note each week on where to lean in next — for you, not a tutor.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this another app that grades my child?

No. Tut-P is built around you, the parent. Instead of another score for your child, you get a weekly Bonding Report that reflects how involved you were — because that involvement is what moves outcomes most.

What exactly happens in a session?

You upload or photograph your child's classwork or homework. Tut-P explains the lesson to you in plain language, gives you talking points to guide your child, and builds a short quiz you go through together. Afterward, it logs the session to your Bonding Dashboard.

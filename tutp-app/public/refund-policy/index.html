Refund Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Our plans

Tut-P plans are bought per child. They do not renew automatically: a plan ends on its last day unless you choose to pay again.

  • Pro: ₹500 for one month (30 days) for one child.
  • Annual Pro: ₹5,000 for one year (365 days) for one child.

Refunds

  • Pro (monthly): no refund once the payment is made.
  • Annual Pro: a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your payment. No refund after 7 days.
  • Any refund ends that plan's access for that child.

How to ask for a refund

Email contact@tutp.online from the email address or phone number you registered with. Include your child's name and the date of the payment. Refunds go back to the original payment method; your bank may take a few working days to show it.

Questions

Write to contact@tutp.online. Tut-P · Hyderabad, India.
